About Dharithri

At Dharithri Manpower Training & Supply Pvt. Ltd., we believe that every person carries hidden potential waiting to be discovered.

Our purpose is not merely to train people—we transform confidence into capability, talent into opportunity, and dreams into meaningful careers. We are committed to identifying genuine talent, nurturing discipline, developing practical skills, and preparing individuals to thrive in today's competitive world.


Every learner who joins Dharithri begins a journey of personal growth, professional excellence, and lifelong learning.


"When one individual grows, an entire family grows. When families grow, society grows."

A young graduate searching for a career... A working professional looking to upgrade skills... A parent wishing for their child's success... Even a grandparent who dreams of seeing their grandchildren build a better future... Everyone has a place in the Dharithri family.
