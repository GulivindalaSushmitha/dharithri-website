About Dharithri Manpower

At Dharithri Manpower Training & Supply Pvt. Ltd., we believe that every person carries hidden potential waiting to be discovered.

Our purpose is not merely to train people—we transform confidence into capability, talent into opportunity, and dreams into meaningful careers. We are committed to identifying genuine talent, nurturing discipline, developing practical skills, and preparing individuals to thrive in today's competitive world.


At Dharithri, every learner is guided through structured training that builds practical skills, professional discipline, and career readiness.

We focus on clear learning outcomes, industry-relevant knowledge, and continuous improvement so that individuals can perform confidently in real workplace roles.

Whether you are a fresh graduate starting your career or a working professional upgrading your skills, Dharithri provides focused training and support to help you move forward with clarity and purpose.
